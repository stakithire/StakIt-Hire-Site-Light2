# StakIt Hire - Box Protection Plan

**Last Updated:** 11 April 2026  
**Plan Fee:** $20.00 (Flat Fee per order)

---

## 1. Overview
The Box Protection Plan is an optional add-on designed to provide peace of mind. It covers the cost of minor, accidental damage to StakIt Hire moving boxes during the rental period.

## 2. What Is Covered
The plan covers the standard **$20.00 damage fee** for up to **three (3) individual moving boxes**. Covered damages include:
*   Small cracks or chips in the plastic.
*   Deep scuffs and scratches from stacking or transport.
*   Minor damage to lids, hinges, or handles.

If the plan is purchased, the customer will not be charged for the first three boxes found to have such damage upon collection inspection.

## 3. What Is NOT Covered
To ensure the plan remains affordable, the following are explicitly excluded from coverage:
*   **Loss or Theft:** Any equipment that is unreturned or stolen ($35 per lost box).
*   **Catastrophic Damage:** Major damage due to misuse or negligence (e.g., boxes returned in pieces, melted, or contaminated with hazardous materials).
*   **Non-Box Equipment:** Damage to trolleys, moving blankets, mattress protectors, or TV sleeves is not covered.
*   **Late Fees:** The plan does not waive fees associated with unready collections or unauthorized rental extensions.

## 4. Operational Process
1.  **Inspection:** All items are counted and inspected upon collection by our logistics team.
2.  **Assessment:** If damage is found, the inspector determines if it falls under "minor damage" as defined above.
3.  **Application:** 
    *   **With Plan:** Fees for the first 3 damaged boxes are waived.
    *   **Without Plan:** The standard $20.00 fee applies to every damaged box from the first item.
